ABOUT BIOMED DIAGNOSTICS

A trusted bridge between global science and Thai healthcare.

Established in Thailand in 2002 as part of Biomed Global, Biomed Diagnostics represents innovative technologies in clinical diagnostics and life science research. We go beyond supplying reagents, instruments and consumables to help customers achieve meaningful results.

✓Scientific expertise — specialist-led consultation and application guidance.
