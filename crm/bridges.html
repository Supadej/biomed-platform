CRM bridge settings

Choose what CRM receives, map IDs, then preview and reconcile. Source systems remain authoritative.

Recommended attributes

SourceRecommended linksHow to use them
Biomed ISCustomer ID, organization, contact, email, phone, billing address, tax ID; product ID, brand, catalogue number, unit; approved quotation, customer order and invoice references.Customer account context, product lookup and order follow-up. Stock balances include warehouse, lot and expiry as snapshots.
Staff PortalStaff ID, name, work email, department, manager; opportunity/tender ID, owner, customer ID, expected value, status; visit dates and service ticket references.Shared identities, assigned sales follow-up, calendars and service history. Map owners explicitly if their source ID differs.
BudgetingFiscal year, product ID, cost center, monthly targets and actual revenue; annual expense budget, actual and commitment.Sales targets and planning context. Map Budget product IDs to CRM portfolio IDs before importing targets.

Passwords, authentication tokens and role grants are never bridged. Financial snapshots do not create invoices, payments or stock transactions. Source deletions are retained as historical snapshots until reviewed.
